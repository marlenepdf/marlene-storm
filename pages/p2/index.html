    'Under Construction' is an exhibition that rethinks design and challenges convention. Designed media are not rigid systems: they can be rebuilt, expanded, or completely reinvented. The exhibition presents the creations of 50 students from the typography and editorial courses "A Book is a Building," "Let's Platform," and "From Groove to Grid" from the 2024/25 winter semester.
